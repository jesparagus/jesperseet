Who Am I?

I am an 18 year old photographer and cinematographer based out of Southern California and I have always loved photography and cinematography. On all the old videos my parents have of me on their camcorder, I can be seen reaching either for the camcorder itself or some other camera. Whether it’s a party or a road trip, wherever I go, my camera follows. Capturing the motion and emotion through still images is a passion that I am determined to hold on to until the end of my life with as much love as I had for it as a child.

In a way, this goal has influenced me. I strive to make photographs that feel timeless, that capture a child-like curiosity in the world. To document places and people and make them feel familiar, as though the viewer has already been to these places, already met these strangers on the street that are in some way shape or form beautiful or unique to me. Elderly people and children I find carry the most emotion, thought, or character but I look for stories in all sorts of people.

I react spontaneously when making images. I turn my camera and instinctually pull focus at a spot of light, a flash of color, hoping to create with the same energy a child would have, no matter the topic, no matter the theme.

I currently attend Chapman University, Dodge College Class of 2030 for Film Production with an emphasis in Cinematography.
